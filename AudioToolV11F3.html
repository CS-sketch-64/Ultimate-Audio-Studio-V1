📖 Audio FX Studio Guide

1. Audio Tracks & Live Mic Setup

Upload a track file OR click 🎤 Enable Microphone Input to sing/talk live into the studio.

🔊 Separate Track & Vocal Mixer: Balance music volume vs microphone volume independently.

🎛️ Mic FX Mode: Choose between Vocal Sliders Active (processed voice) and Clean Voice Bypass.

💡 Pro Tip: Works best with headphones! Wearing headphones during live monitoring prevents acoustic echo and feedback loops from your speakers back into the mic.

2. Independent Track FX vs Vocal FX

Track FX: Apply pitch shifting, 8D audio, wobble, reverb, and EQ strictly to the backing track.

Vocal FX: Apply pitch shifting, vocal harmonizer chorus, delay, reverb, and EQ strictly to your live microphone input.

3. Live Custom Soundboard (Keys 1 – 6)

Upload up to 6 custom sound effects (.mp3 or .wav). Press keyboard keys 1 through 6 anytime during playback or singing to fire off sound drops live!

4. Recording & Exporting

Click Record & Download to live record your audio track, vocal performance, and soundboard drops simultaneously into one clean audio file.
